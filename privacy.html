PRIVACY POLICY · SEPTEMBER 13, 2026

Your focus stays yours.

Tamiyoka is designed to work without a Tamiyoka account, advertising, or analytics tracking.

Information stored on your Mac

Tamiyoka stores your companion state, task names, focus history, daily goals, and preferences locally inside the app’s macOS sandbox. This information is used to provide the timer, history, streak, rewards, and companion features.

Information we collect

Tamiyoka does not send your focus history, task names, companion progress, or preferences to the publisher. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Purchases

Apple processes Tamiyoka’s in-app purchases. The app reads Apple-verified transaction information to determine whether Premium access is active. The publisher does not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and are used to tell you when a focus session or break finishes. Tamiyoka does not include your task name in notification text.

Deleting local information

You can remove Tamiyoka’s local information by deleting its sandbox data from your Mac. Removing local app data does not cancel an active subscription. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple Account.

Children’s privacy

Tamiyoka does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes and support

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated. For privacy questions or app support, open a support request. GitHub issues are public, so do not include sensitive personal information.
